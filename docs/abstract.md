# Sudbury Slice: Abstract
 **By:** Rishabh Bhutani
 
 **The problem.** 
 Customers struggle to read the menu, check toppings, and choose a pizza because there are no ratings.

 **Who it is for.** 
Workers looking for food after their shifts, people who are having a party at home, anyone who's hungry, somebody who wants a late-night snack.

 **Why it matters.** 
 Customers can clearly see the menu; they won't have to call the store to confirm an unclear menu every time. This will reduce the burden of extra phone calls on Moose's shoulders. This may also increase sales because customers can understand the menu easily instead of choosing another store.

 **What the app must remember.** 
One customer can rate several pizzas.
One pizza can receive ratings from several customers. 
Each rating connects a customer to a pizza and stores 1–5 stars with an optional comment.
A pizza can also have several toppings, and the same topping can appear on different pizzas.
