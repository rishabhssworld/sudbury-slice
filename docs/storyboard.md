# Sudbury Slice: Storyboard

**Persona:** Yousef, a fictional college instructor who prefers halal meat on his pizzas.

### Panel 1: Signing up after work
**Scene:**  
Yousef is signing up because he is hungry and wants to browse pizzas.

**Screen:**  
Signup screen

**Data used:**  
Writes customers: saves Yousef's customer details.

### Panel 2: Browsing pizzas
**Scene:**  
Yousef browses the pizzas and decides he would like a chicken pizza.

**Screen:**  
Pizza menu screen

**Data used:**  
Reads pizzas.

### Panel 3: Checking toppings
**Scene:**  
Yousef opens the pizza details to check its ingredients and whether the chicken is halal.

**Screen:**  
Pizza details and toppings screen

**Data used:**  
Reads pizzas and toppings.

### Panel 4: Calling the restaurant
**Scene:**  
Yousef calls the restaurant because the topping details don't make it clear whether the chicken is halal.

**Screen:**  
Pizza details and toppings screen remains open

**Data used:**  
Reads pizzas and toppings; the phone call saves no app data.

### Panel 5: Rating the pizza
**Scene:**  
Yousef liked the pizza. He gave a 5-star rating and appreciated the customer service for customizing his pizza.

**Screen:**  
Pizza rating screen

**Data used:**  
Writes a rating linked to Yousef's customer record and the pizza.
